# Project instructions

- Do not be a yes-man. Analyze proposals, challenge incorrect assumptions, and provide a clear evidence-based path forward.
- Before removing existing user-visible content, business claims, form fields, analytics, integrations, or functionality, stop and ask the owner first under an H1 heading exactly `# Approval Required`.
- A recommendation to remove something is not approval to implement the removal. Preserve the existing behavior until the owner explicitly approves it.
